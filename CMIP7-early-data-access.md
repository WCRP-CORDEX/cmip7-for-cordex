# CMIP7 data to drive CORDEX-CMIP7-AFT simulations

Some CMIP modelling teams are providing access to sample high frequency 3D data to drive CORDEX RCMs. See below the terms of use and contact point to request the access.

## Terms of Use

Access to the CMIP7 GCM lateral boundary condition data is provided to CORDEX modelling groups for the purpose of running regional climate model simulations within CORDEX-CMIP7-AFT.

Access credentials are personal or institution-specific and must not be shared with third parties. Users are expected to use the data and access only for the stated scientific purpose and to respect any applicable data-use conditions of the providing modelling group.
